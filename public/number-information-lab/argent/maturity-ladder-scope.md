# Maturity ladder public scope

This public data file exposes only the bounded 2026-2032 maturity ladder derived from Agence France Trésor detailed outstanding-title pages.

Scope rules:

- values are published face / nominal outstanding amounts in EUR billions;
- OAT, OAT€i and OATi are aggregated by maturity year;
- inflation-linked outstanding amounts are not exact future redemption cash flows;
- the snapshot is reconciled through issues settled on 24 August 2026;
- the 3 September 2026 long-term OAT auction settles on 7 September 2026 and is excluded from this settled snapshot;
- maturity does not imply automatic refinancing;
- this is not total French public debt and not total State financing need.
